# F1: 中国神话/山海经系手游的品牌定位

## Findings

### [1] Black Myth: Wukong（Game Science，2024-08-20）以「西游记续写 + 写实神话景观」为品牌核心，IGN 评价其 setting「refreshingly unique and steeped in rich Chinese culture」，但叙事强依赖原著先验知识
- quote: "the setting is refreshingly unique and steeped in rich Chinese culture"
- url: https://www.ign.com/articles/black-myth-wukong-review
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2024-08-20 (updated 2024-10-11)
- confidence: high

### [2] Black Myth: Wukong 每章结尾使用「完全不同的艺术风格」动画 vignette（绘本/定格/动画）作为品牌记忆点；音乐用 epic drum + 笛/磬 建立东方听觉隐喻
- quote: "each of the six self-contained chapters culminates with a stunningly gorgeous animated vignette… done in a completely different art style, with one drawn to look like a storybook, another using stop-motion animation, and another done in the style of an anime"
- url: https://www.ign.com/articles/black-myth-wukong-review
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2024-08-20
- confidence: high

### [3] Eurogamer 指出《Black Myth: Wukong》失败面：视觉奇观强但「disjointed storytelling」「lack of tonal variety」导致章节转折 jarring、探索方向感丧失——说明仅靠美术隐喻、缺少 UI/叙事与世界观绑定会削弱品牌
- quote: "you'll finish one chapter fighting a flaming bear on a top of a hill one minute, then immediately start the next lying half-dead in a desert… with zero context"
- url: https://www.eurogamer.net/black-myth-wukong-review
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2024-08-20
- confidence: high

### [4] Black Myth: Wukong 三天销量 1000 万套，成为中国首款被行业认定的 AAA；此后带动 Phantom Blade Zero、Tides of Annihilation、Wuchang 等「中国神话/武侠动作 RPG」品牌集群
- quote: "It sold 10m copies in just three days, and remains the second most played game on Steam… Wukong is now considered the country's first AAA release."
- url: https://www.eurogamer.net/how-black-myth-wukong-has-changed-the-game-for-chinese-developers
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2025-05-13
- confidence: high

### [5] Phantom Blade Zero 的品牌定位自造词「kung-fu punk」：把中国武侠文化与全球时尚/蒸汽朋克/赛博朋克混搭，导演 Liang 明确「降低文化门槛」（revenge/loyalty/love 通用主题），并要求玩家「不需要做中国文化的功课」
- quote: "kung-fu punk", mixing traditional Chinese culture with "modern and fashion culture globally"… "they don't need to do any homework on Chinese cultures to understand the stories"
- url: https://www.eurogamer.net/how-black-myth-wukong-has-changed-the-game-for-chinese-developers
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2025-05-13
- confidence: high

### [6] Phantom Blade Zero 团队用 3D 扫描古建筑换取政府投资；其品牌哲学是「表面可去中国化、内核必须是中国的」——对照宫崎骏/小岛秀夫「表面科幻/西幻、内核仍是日本」
- quote: "they are presenting some philosophies of our thinking, which is Chinese" / "use of 3D scans of ancient Chinese buildings" with Chinese government investment
- url: https://www.eurogamer.net/how-black-myth-wukong-has-changed-the-game-for-chinese-developers
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2025-05-13
- confidence: high

### [7] 《山海经》异兽图像的核心是「嵌合体式形体重组」（加减、夸张、位移、拼贴），可作为品牌视觉隐喻的方法论：从形仿→意仿→文化仿三层递进（Nature HSSC，2026）
- quote: "they employed deconstructive techniques—such as addition, subtraction, exaggeration, displacement, collage… ‘Yingzhao’ is described as having ‘a human face, horse body, bird wings, and tiger stripes.’"
- url: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41599-026-07885-7
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-06-30
- confidence: high

### [8] 现代插画《观山海》（Shan Ze）与剪纸版《山海海经》证明：丹青/水墨/剪纸等「国风媒介语言」可把山海经神话重构进当代艺术语言，直接对应「丹青录」类博物馆美学的品牌路径
- quote: "in the Guan Shan Hai (Viewing the Classic of Mountains and Seas) series, illustrator Shan Ze reconstructs Chinese painting styles with modern esthetics, infusing ancient mythology into contemporary artistic language"
- url: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41599-026-07885-7
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-06-30
- confidence: high

### [9] 「新神话」动画（《大鱼海棠》《哪吒》）的品牌手法是：重构神话符号链，把海洋从「可征服的威胁」改写为「共生的自然」，并与国风水墨笔触/古典音乐节奏融合——视觉隐喻必须承载现代价值观，而非仅换皮
- quote: "they shift the symbolic signified from ‘repressive nature’ to ‘symbiotic nature’… aesthetically fuse waves, water patterns, and spirit pearls with ‘National Style’ symbols such as traditional ink brush techniques and classical musical rhythms"
- url: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41599-026-07885-7
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-06-30
- confidence: high

### [10] 文博数字化 THEB 模型（Thing-Human-Emotion-Beauty）证明：AI 生成内容能显著提升 engagement/satisfaction/immersion 与审美共鸣，但**不能**显著提升历史深度——丹青录若走博物馆叙事，必须补足「物—人—情—美」四维，而非只堆视觉
- quote: "while historical depth was not significantly enhanced, the AI video substantially improved engagement, satisfaction, and immersion, particularly in aesthetic appreciation and emotional resonance"
- url: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-25843-2
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-11-25
- confidence: high

### [11] 《原神》代表另一条成功路径：中国工作室做「多国文化混搭开放世界」（Teyvat），品牌不锚定单一山海经/中国神话，靠探索密度与角色抽卡驱动；IGN 给 9/10 Editors' Choice，叙事被评为最弱点
- quote: "Genshin Impact's fantastical world, wide array of diverse playable characters, and deep RPG systems are all unique and awesome… its story is one of its weaker points"
- url: https://www.ign.com/articles/genshin-impact-review
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2020-09-27 (updated 2022-11-04)
- confidence: high

### [12] 中国游戏市场 2025 年约 518 亿美元（Niko Partners），预计 2026 年 539 亿、2030 年 598 亿；原神等常青 live-service 与新游 Where Winds Meet（上线 24h 超 200 万玩家）共同驱动——神话题材手游处于高增长市场中
- quote: "in 2025, the Chinese market generated around $51.8bn in revenue… rising 4 percent in 2026 to $53.9bn… reach $59.8bn [by 2030]"
- url: https://www.eurogamer.net/china-games-market-50-billion-dollars-value
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2026-09-07
- confidence: high

### [13] 游戏本地化研究显示：Black Myth / Sekiro 的英文本地化比 RDR2 **更复杂**（更高词汇熵与依存距离），挑战「翻译必然简化」假设——中国神话游戏的文本/术语本地化可选择保留复杂度以强化异质性品牌
- quote: "the localized games, Black Myth and Sekiro, demonstrate greater lexical complexity and comparable or greater syntactic complexity relative to Red Dead Redemption 2"
- url: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41599-025-06424-0
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-12-24
- confidence: high

## Dead ends
- WebSearch 工具不可用（平台未启用插件），无法做开放网络检索
- Wikipedia（en/zh）、Google、Steam Store、Baidu Baike、GameLook、arxiv.org 均 transport error 或 403/404
- Bing 中国区对中文游戏查询返回无关结果（抖音/拼音字典/韩剧），英文查询亦被污染
- 未能直接获取国内山海经系手游（妄想山海 / 山海镜花 / 忘川风华录 / 物华弥新 / 花亦山心之月）的官方品牌文档或 TapTap/官网页面
- GameRes 站内搜索为前端渲染，爬取返回空壳
