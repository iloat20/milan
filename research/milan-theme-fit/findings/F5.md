# F5: 替代主题概念库与迁移成本

## Findings

### [1] 成功手游品牌命名范式可归纳为「物质锚点 + 组织载体 + 角色本体论」三层，且必须同时咬合经济系统与叙事危机，而非只做展柜式陈列。
- quote: "Arknights was originally planned to be named Archknights, but this was changed so the name would be a combination of \"Ark\" (as in Noah's Ark of the Bible) and \"Knights.\" The \"knight\" part is also meant to be synonymous with \"Night.\" Its symbolism is to denote the game's overall serious tone by depicting the characters as \"Knights of the Ark\" who guard the lives aboard in the long \"night.\""
- url: https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Arknights
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2026-09-07 (last edited)
- confidence: high

### [2] 明日方舟用「源石 Originium」把能源、魔法、传染病、货币、身份认同压成同一物质，使「抽卡/养成」与「世界危机」共用一套隐喻；这是「物质锚点」范式的最强证据。
- quote: "Originium (/əˈɹɪd͡ʒɪniəm/; Chinese: 源石; Pinyin: Yuán shí; lit. \"Source/Origin Stone\") is a substance in the world of Arknights which forms after Catastrophes and is widely used as an energy source, but causes the development of Oripathy in living organisms. ... Originium allows the use of various forms of \"magic\" known as Originium Arts"
- url: https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Originium
- source_type: secondary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [4] 官方商品层把同一物质拆成三级货币命名（至纯源石 / 合成玉 / 源石碎片），说明「主题物质」在落地时必须给出可翻译的充值/抽卡/碎片三档词，否则品牌叙事会与商店脱节。

Carrying the title of Timekeeper, Vertin's agenda is filled with goals of her own, as well as those set by the St. Pavlov Foundation. The player follows Vertin's steps as she travels through different time periods, attempting to salvage as much as possible before each era gets sifted out by the Storm."

### [10] 对 Milan「裂隙撕裂 Shinwa/Aether/Ironveil 三界 + 重聚原初之环」叙事，可迁移范式的适配度排序为：物质锚点（源石式）≈ 英灵/从者召唤（FGO式）> 时间抢救（1999式）> 神话凭依/代行者（Dislyte式）> 展柜/图鉴/丹青典藏（被动陈列，最弱）。理由：前三者把玩家职责定义为「行动」（供能/召唤/抢救），而展柜隐喻把玩家定义为「观看」，与「重聚环之碎片」的动词冲突。
- quote: "Originium will become the beacon, condensed by our civilization... If one day, other lifeforms in the universe lose their homeland and seek a way to resolve their plight, they will witness... that we once shined, we once resisted, and this is where we rest."

### [11] 高迁移品牌词的共同结构是「一个可数名词同时是货币、身份与危机产物」（源石/英灵/神秘学家），而不是「一个场所名词」（馆/录/典藏）。因此 Milan 若保留「原初之环」为核心目标，应把「环的碎片/环痕/残玦」升格为可数名词与货币词，而非把品牌定在存放它们的场所上。
- quote: "The most valuable of all Originium products is the Originite Prime – highly refined, pure Originium which serves as the main energy source throughout the world."
- url: https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Originium
- source_type: secondary
- published: unknown
- confidence: medium

### [12] Arknights 中文主名「明日方舟」并非早期确定，而是在出版截止压力下由团队成员从俗语「择日不如撞日」临场提出；说明成功品牌名可后置、可迭代，不必把当前「丹青录」当作不可替换的锁定资产。
- quote: "The English name \"Arknights\" was the first one to be confirmed during the game's development, but the Chinese name \"明日方舟\" was determined later when a member in the development team proposed it during a rather typical barbecue party. ... According to that person, he got this idea from this Chinese proverb, 择日不如撞日 (to do it right now instead of choosing other days), as they were running out of time to meet the publication deadline."
- url: https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Arknights
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2026-09-07 (last edited)
- confidence: high

## Dead ends
- built-in websearch tool was unavailable for the whole session (\"Web search unavailable\"); used Bing HTML via webfetch instead.
- Fandom wikis (Genshin/HSR/FGO/Arknights fandom, dislyte.fandom.com) consistently failed with Transport error / SSRF.
- en.wikipedia.org Transport error for Genshin / FGO / Arknights / HSR.
- HoyoLab wiki and genshin.hoyoverse.com / hsr.hoyoverse.com returned 403 or empty SPA shells; no Vision/Teyvat/Aeon/Path primary text obtained.
- biligame wikis (hsr 星神/命途, 忘川风华录 wc, 阴阳师 yys, 碧蓝档案 ba, 来古弥新 lgmx, reverse1999) returned HTTP 567; HSR and 忘川风华录 primary lore not fetched.
- Bing search for 忘川风华录 was URL-mangled to bilibili.com content; no usable 忘川 result.
- atlasacademy.io/db/ 404; fgo.wiki 职阶 404 (职阶 content still visible via 特性:从者 page).
- Reverse:1999 official re1999.bluepoch.com is a JS shell with no lore text; Storm/Timekeeper claims rest on Prydwen secondary (reputable, not official).
