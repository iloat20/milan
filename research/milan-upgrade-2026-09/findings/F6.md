## Dead ends
- No local InspectionScreen / InspectionActivity equivalent under Kotlin UI; product docs still describe inspection as core, but the current app surface does not expose it.
- Web searches were unnecessary: local specs, @Deprecated markers, and nav comments already establish designed-vs-built status.
- Older plans (2026-08-09 experience retention, 2026-07-29/30 UI redesign) are historical and partially superseded by the 2026-09 wiring/cleanup work; used only for product-identity evidence, not current status.

## Suggested follow-ups
- Should inspection be rebuilt as a dedicated screen, or folded into CharacterDetail as a true interactive hero stage (gesture + actions + photo)?
- Is Dungeon/abyss intentionally retired, or should it be rewired as the missing mid-core PvE loop between Story and Tower?
- For monetization, is the product staying offline-only (then wire monthly card as local premium), or preparing for store distribution (then charge must not ship as fake billing)?
